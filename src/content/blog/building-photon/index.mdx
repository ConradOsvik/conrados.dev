## Why rewrite?

The old Django backend served us well for years, but as our team grew and requirements evolved, we needed something that better fit our workflow.

## The stack

We settled on **Hono** as the HTTP framework, **Drizzle ORM** for type-safe database access, and **Postgres** as the database. Redis handles caching and session management.

## Lessons learned

Migrating a production system used by hundreds of students is no small feat. The key was running both systems in parallel during the transition period.
